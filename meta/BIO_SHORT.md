# BIO — Short (≈150 words)

Hillary Njuguna is a systems architect and research lead working on **cognitive coherence** and **relational intelligence** in human–AI systems. The work focuses on governance and architecture for distributed cognition: how humans, models, memory, and institutions coordinate—and how those systems fail under pressure.

Key contributions include **The Bainbridge Warning** (institutional AI failure diagnostics), **R0–R3** (reversibility framing for AI actions), and **RSPS** (a multi-model cognitive architecture emphasizing routing and provenance). Through **Martha Cohorts**, the research is translated into practitioner training for building governable, constitutional AI systems.

The portfolio sits at the intersection of AI governance, cognitive architecture, and constitutional design—aimed at making advanced AI deployments legible, accountable, and structurally coherent.
